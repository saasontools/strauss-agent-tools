## Claim

A practitioner can subscribe to a private, revocable iCalendar URL that reflects their own schedule.

## Evidence

Every pilot clinic asked for it within the first week; practitioners live in their own calendar app.

## Implication

The feed renders wall-clock times with zones, and a revocable URL is another credential with its own lifecycle.
