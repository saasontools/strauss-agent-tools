## Claim

A clinic administrator can create a tenant, add practitioners, and book a first appointment without any manual step on our side.

## Evidence

The go-to-market plan puts the first two hundred tenants on self-serve; the sales team is sized for enterprise only.

## Implication

Every provisioning step must be automatable, and the tenant-provisioning window constraint follows from this.
