## Risk

applyAnchorPatch accepts a remove that takes the last anchor: against one stored anchor, `{reason, remove:[{file:'a.ts'}]}` returns `{anchors: [], changes:[{op:'remove',...}]}`, and updateAnchors publishes the empty array. The reviewer gate allows it — anchor-update is in WRITE_VERBS with no authorship test, unlike status and answer, which call recordAuthor in ruleFor — so agent:security can empty a record generated by a human or by agent:claude. Nothing downstream names the result: validate.ts reports type, superseded_by, backlink, supersedes, links, anchors' internal shape and sources, never a record with zero anchors, and detectDrift iterates strauss_anchors, so an empty list yields no entry at all.

## Why it matters

standing, verified[] and the body are untouched by design, so the record still reads as evidence someone checked, while the pointer that made it checkable is gone and the drift report stays silent. A replacement is self-policing — the kept hash drifts against the new location — but a removal has nothing left to drift.

## Mitigation

None in the diff. Refuse a patch whose result is empty, or report an anchorless record in validate, or give anchor-update the ownership test status and answer already get.

## Verification

applyAnchorPatch('decision.x', [{file:'a.ts',symbol:'S',hash:'sha256:'+'a'.repeat(64)}], {reason:'r', remove:[{file:'a.ts'}]}) returns anchors: [] rather than throwing.
